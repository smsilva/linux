# Work in progress

The owner's pillar for every project (2026-10-29; raised from 1 to 3 the same day): **at most three issues In Progress at a time**, per person, on every board.

- With three items In Progress, finish one, or give it back (to `Todo`, with a handoff comment), before taking another one.
- A parent whose sub-issues are the work stays out of In Progress; only the sub-issues being worked on are In Progress.
- When a board shows more than three items In Progress for the same person, say so and ask which ones stay; release the others before starting.
- The board's own script enforces it where one exists (Nereus: `scripts/board/queue claim` refuses a fourth item), as the deterministic pillar asks.
